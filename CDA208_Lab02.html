Explore the architectural beauty of India

1. Badrinath Temple

Location

Chamoli, Uttarakhand, India

Significance

Nothern anchor of India's four cardinal pilgrimages sites, alongside Dwarka(West), Puri(East), and Rameshwaram(South)

Travel Details

Best time to visit: May to June (peak season with pleasant weather)
Nearest Airport: Jolly Grant Airport
Nearest Railway Station: Rishikesh
Opening Hours: 4:30 AM to 1:00 PM and from 4:00 PM to 9:00 PM

2. Dilwara Temple

Location

Chamoli, Uttarakhand, India

Significance

Regarded as one of the most sacred Maha Tirths in the world

Travel Details

Best time to visit : October to March
Nearest Airport : Udaipur
Nearest Railway Station: Abu Road
Opening Hours: 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM

3. kapaleeshwarar-temple

Location

Mylapore neighborhood of Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

Significance

7th-century landmark of Dravidian architecture dedicated to Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati.

Travel Details

Best time to visit: November to April
Nearest Airport: Chennai International Airport
Nearest Railway Station : Mundakanni Amman Kovil MRTS Station
Opening Hours: 4:30 AM to 1:00 PM and from 4:00 PM to 9:00 PM

Location

Chamoli, Uttarakhand, India

Significance

one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva.

Travel Details

5. Khajuraho Temple

Location

Chhatarpur district, northern Madhya Pradesh, central India.

Significance

Designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Travel Details

Nearest Railway Station: Khajuraho Railway Station

6. Konark Sun Temple

Location

On the coastline of Puri district in Odisha, India

Significance

It represents the absolute pinnacle and culmination of traditional Kalinga (Orissan) architecture. It was designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1984

Travel Details

Best time to visit: October to February
Nearest Airport: Biju Patnaik International Airport (BBI) in Bhubaneswar,
Nearest Railway Station: Puri Railway Station (PURI)
Opening Hours: 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM
