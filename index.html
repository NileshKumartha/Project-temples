🏛️ Explore the Architectural Beauty of India 🏛️

1. Badrinath Temple

📍 Location

Chamoli, Uttarakhand, India

⭐ Significance

Northern anchor of India's four cardinal pilgrimage sites, alongside Dwarka, Puri, and Rameshwaram.

✈️ Travel Details

Best time: May to June
Airport: Jolly Grant Airport
Railway: Rishikesh
Hours: 4:30 AM–1:00 PM and 4:00 PM–9:00 PM

2. Dilwara Temple

📍 Location

Mount Abu, Rajasthan, India

⭐ Significance

One of the most sacred Maha Tirths in the world and an important Jain pilgrimage site.

✈️ Travel Details

Best time: October to March
Airport: Udaipur
Railway: Abu Road
Hours: 12:00 PM–5:00 PM

3. Kapaleeshwarar Temple

📍 Location

Mylapore, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India

⭐ Significance

A 7th-century landmark of Dravidian architecture dedicated to Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati.

✈️ Travel Details

Best time: November to April
Airport: Chennai International Airport
Railway: Mundakanni Amman Kovil MRTS Station
Hours: 4:30 AM–1:00 PM and 4:00 PM–9:00 PM

4. Kedarnath Temple

📍 Location

Rudraprayag, Uttarakhand, India

⭐ Significance

One of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva.

✈️ Travel Details

Best time: May to June or September to October
Airport: Jolly Grant Airport
Railway: Rishikesh
Hours: 4:00 AM–9:00 PM

5. Khajuraho Temple

📍 Location

Chhatarpur district, Madhya Pradesh, India

⭐ Significance

A UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its intricate medieval Hindu and Jain architecture.

✈️ Travel Details

Best time: October to March
Airport: Khajuraho Airport
Railway: Khajuraho Railway Station
Hours: 6:00 AM–6:00 PM

6. Konark Sun Temple

📍 Location

Puri district, Odisha, India

⭐ Significance

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and pinnacle of traditional Kalinga architecture.

✈️ Travel Details

Best time: October to February
Airport: Biju Patnaik International Airport
Railway: Puri Railway Station
Hours: 6:00 AM–8:00 PM

7. Mahabaleshwar Temple

📍 Location

Old Mahabaleshwar, Maharashtra, India

⭐ Significance

A revered Shiva temple whose spiritual merit is traditionally compared with the twelve Jyotirlingas.

✈️ Travel Details

Best time: October to February
Airport: Pune International Airport
Railway: Pune Railway Station
Hours: 6:00 AM–8:00 PM
